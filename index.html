About

I work at the intersection of machine learning, time-series forecasting, and computational energy research, with a particular interest in developing data-driven models for complex real-world systems. My research has focused on two complementary problems: improving long-term electricity demand forecasting and accelerating the design and optimisation of perovskite solar cells. For my thesis, I investigate lead-free perovskite solar-cell structures using device-level simulation and machine learning to understand and optimise their photovoltaic performance. The work is conducted under the supervision of Mohona Das Gupta , Lecturer, Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST).

I have hands-on experience with SCAPS-1D for solar-cell device simulation and optimisation, alongside Python, NumPy, Pandas, scikit-learn, TensorFlow/Keras, and Darts for machine-learning and time-series modelling. My forecasting work includes models such as TiDE, TFT, TSMixer, N-BEATS, N-HiTS, XGBoost, Random Forest, and linear models, while my recent research involves developing modified deep-learning architectures for improved forecasting. On the photovoltaic side, I work with multilayer perovskite device structures, parameter optimisation, performance evaluation, and ML-based prediction of photovoltaic characteristics.

I am seeking a funded graduate research position starting in 2027, particularly in machine learning, time-series forecasting, computational energy systems, or AI-assisted materials and photovoltaic research.
